How to beat scalpers

  • Want one card? Buy the single. Ripping packs to chase it usually costs far more than buying it outright.
  • Check what it sold for, not what it's listed at. Use the sold-price links. Asking prices can be anything.
  • Wait out the hype. New sets and hot products are often priciest in the first weeks, and reprints can bring prices down.
  • Buy sealed at store price. Big stores and your local game store sell at MSRP when they have stock. Ask when restocks come in.
  • Walk away from pressure. "Last one, price goes up tomorrow" is a sales trick. There's always another card.
  • Trade with collectors, not flippers. Fair trades keep the hobby fun. Use the 🤝 Trade tab to check.

Where the prices come from

Card info and prices come from free, open community databases. Prices update about once a day and are estimates, not offers.

  • Pokémon: TCGdex (TCGplayer market prices plus Cardmarket averages)
  • Disney Lorcana: Lorcast (daily market prices)
  • One Piece: OPTCG API (TCGplayer market prices and 2-week history)
  • Card scanner: Tesseract.js, which reads the photo on your device. Your photos are never uploaded.

Some "buy" links are affiliate links: if you buy through them, Binder Buddy may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. It helps keep the app free.

Privacy, plainly

Your binder, photos, wishlist and settings are stored in this browser on this device only. When you search, scan or update prices, the app asks the databases above about a card name or number, and nothing about you. The first scan downloads the free scanner program from jsDelivr. Back up now and then, because clearing your browser data erases your binder.
